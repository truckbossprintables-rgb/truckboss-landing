Questions

Straight answers.

No fine print, no runaround. If something's not here, the trial answers the rest.

Is TruckBoss really free to start?

Yes — 14 days free on any plan, no credit card. You get every feature during the trial; nothing's locked behind a bigger plan.

Do I need to be good with computers?

No. If you can use a phone, you can use TruckBoss. Setup takes a few minutes and walks you through your truck and your costs step by step.

Does it work on my phone, in the cab?

Yes. It runs in any phone browser — no app store, no install. Punch in a load offer at the truck stop and get your answer before you call the broker back.

How does it know if a load is actually profitable?

It uses live diesel prices along your route, your truck's real fuel mileage and fixed costs, and your target rate per mile — then shows your net profit and whether to take it. No napkin math.

Can it help with IFTA and taxes?

Yes. It tracks IFTA miles by state, logs fuel and expense receipts as you go, and keeps it organized for Schedule C — so tax time isn't a shoebox of paper.

What does it cost after the free trial?

Plans are priced by how many trucks you run, starting with a single owner-operator plan. Founding members lock in today's price for life — see pricing.

Do I have to sign a contract?

No. It's month to month, cancel anytime, and your data is always yours to export.

I already use the TruckBoss printables — is this the same thing?

Same team, next step. The app does automatically what the printables do by hand — the load math, the logs, the reminders — all in one place.
